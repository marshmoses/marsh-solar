What we supply

Solar products for every project

From a single panel to a complete hybrid system, we stock certified equipment with warranties you can rely on.

Solar Panels

Mono and poly crystalline modules from 100W to 550W+ for rooftop, ground-mount and off-grid arrays.

Solar Inverters

Amizar hybrid and off-grid inverters from 2.2 kVA to 3.2 kVA with built-in MPPT, pure sine wave output and LCD display.

Solar Batteries

Amizar LiFePO4 deep-cycle batteries — 12.8V 100Ah (1280Wh) with 50A max charge/discharge. CE and UN38.3 certified.

Solar Water Heaters

Aquasun evacuated-tube systems in 200L and 300L capacities — pressurized tanks with 20 or 30 collector tubes.

Cables & Wires

UV-rated twin-core DC solar cable in red and black, sold by the coil. MC4 connectors and earthing kits also available.

Charge Controllers

MPPT solar charge controllers and Aquasun digital heater controllers with LCD display, timing and temperature settings.

Mounting Accessories

Aluminium roof rails, mid/end clamps, ground structures, tilt kits and stainless fasteners — bulk stock available.

Complete Solar Systems

Turnkey kits combining panels, Amizar inverters, LiFePO4 batteries and cabling — sized for homes, shops and off-grid sites.

Electrical Accessories

Solar LED floodlights, immersion elements, breakers, combiners, surge protection and changeover switches.
